Overview

As a Sensor Fusion Research Intern, I built a multimodal sensing stack for wearable research: synchronized EMG and IMU measurements that stayed honest under real human-motion trials.

The mission was end-to-end ownership of the sensing path—architecture, calibration, estimation support for an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF), and experimental validation with motion-capture and arm-movement studies— without waiting on a finished pipeline to inherit.

What I Did

01 | Sensing Architecture

Systems

Built a multimodal stack on a Teensy 4.0 for synchronized EMG and IMU capture. Wrote Python pipelines for 1 kHz acquisition without dropped samples.

02 | Estimation & Fusion

Algorithms

Collected and prepared IMU and EMG datasets from controlled motion-capture and arm-movement trials to support EKF multi-sensor state estimation, then analyzed results against fusion accuracy targets.

03 | Calibration

Data

Designed signal conditioning for MMG filtering and hard/soft-iron IMU alignment. Checked timing sync so measurements stayed high-fidelity across channels.

04 | Validation

Rigor

Ran human-subject testing across motion-capture and arm-movement conditions. Used Python for filtering, signal processing, and statistical checks on the experimental data.

Scope | Summer 2025

This was a sensor fusion research internship: multimodal sensing, calibration, estimation support, and experimental validation. The research value was building and proving a measurement system under real human-motion conditions.
