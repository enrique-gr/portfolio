Coursework Projects § 2.0

Selected projects from coursework, each representing a distinct systems engineering exercise.

Regulation Foosball Table

CW-01
SolidWorksDFM

Bottom-up CAD assembly of a fully playable regulation foosball table — 200+ parts with full mate constraints, design tables for parametric variants, and a tolerance stack-up analysis verifying assembly clearances. Final file: 128 MB.

I also ran a DFM review pass, identifying parts that would require redesign for injection molding vs. machining — a good exercise in thinking about how manufacturing constraints shape design decisions from the start.

Adaptive Cruise Control System

CW-02
MATLABSimulink

Simulated longitudinal vehicle control using PID loops and radar-based headway logic in MATLAB/Simulink. I built a linearized plant model of the vehicle dynamics, tuned the controllers against step and ramp reference inputs, and validated performance across a range of operating conditions including grade changes and lead-vehicle braking scenarios.

RF Priority System for Emergency Vehicles

CW-03
ArduinoRF Comms

Wireless traffic preemption system for emergency vehicles using Arduino and RF communications. I implemented a multi-node signaling protocol with fail-safe fallback — if the RF link is lost, signals default to a safe state rather than staying green indefinitely.

Landing Gear FEA

CW-04
ANSYSFEA

Structural analysis of a nose landing gear designed to withstand 3 g hard-landing impact loads using ANSYS static structural. I ran topology optimization on the initial design and achieved 18% mass reduction while staying within yield-safety margins across the full load envelope.
