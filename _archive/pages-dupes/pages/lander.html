About the Project

This is a real-time physics engine simulating a 1D vertical landing scenario. The system uses a standard PID controller to modulate thrust vectoring against gravity and stochastic wind disturbances.

The visualizer on the right is currently running in Autopilot Mode to demonstrate the stabilization logic.
