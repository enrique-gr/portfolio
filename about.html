Approach

Engineering principles

The habits that show up most in how I approach problems.

I try to understand what the system is actually being asked to do before jumping into tools, opinions, or complexity. That usually makes better decisions possible much earlier.

Hidden assumptions create fragile systems. I would rather expose them early, write them down clearly, and let testing decide whether they survive.

Logs, measurements, and behavior matter more than what the design was supposed to do. Staying close to data keeps the work honest and cuts through ego quickly.

Good engineering is not only about solving your own part well. It is also about making interfaces cleaner, documentation clearer, and collaboration easier for everyone else.
