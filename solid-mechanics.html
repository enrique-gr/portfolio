Landing Gear Analysis

Coursework (Team Project - Fall 2025)

Overview

Nose landing gear under a hard-landing case: define a clean load path, compute stresses, compare failure modes, then check the hand analysis with FEA.

What I Did

01 | System Simplification

Model

Strut as beam in bending/compression; axle as shaft under combined shear/bending via beam theory.

02 | Loading & Assumptions

Loads

Conservative 3g impact with fixed trunnion support and wheel/axle point loads.

03 | Material Selection

Materials

Al 7075-T6 strut and 4340 steel axle with tabulated allowables.

04 | Failure Criteria

Safety

Von Mises yield, pin/bolt shear, strut buckling checks, and component safety factors.

05 | FEA Validation

ANSYS

ANSYS check of peak stress locations, especially axle–strut interfaces and discontinuities.

Scope | Solid Mechanics

Simplified nose-gear assembly for coursework. Hand analysis first, FEA as a check that assumptions stayed conservative.
