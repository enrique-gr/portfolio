Towed-Sensor Winch

Coursework (MEMS 4110 | ME Design | Active)

Overview

Senior design for the AIAA Design/Build/Fly 2026–27 Mission 3 theme: a motorized winch that deploys a towed sensor on dual steel cables, switches light modes through the tow path, and docks on retract.

Work spans mechanical design, electronics, and embedded control on one mechatronics stack.

What I Did

01 | Mechanical Design

Mech

Printed frame, drum, and a streamlined sensor body sized for stable tow and reliable docking.

02 | Electronics

Power

Battery, motor driver, MCUs, and a slip ring so power and light commands ride the tow path.

03 | Embedded Control

Control

Encoder length feedback with commands from a laptop or RC transmitter.

04 | Mission Goals

Ops

Deploy and retract to a set length, keep the sensor stable while towed, and switch light modes on command.

Scope | MEMS 4110

Active senior design. Bench prototype proves deploy, retract, and lights; final print is in progress.
