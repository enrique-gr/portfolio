Towed-Sensor Winch

Coursework (MEMS 4110 | ME Design | Active)

Mechatronics Brief

Senior design build for the AIAA Design/Build/Fly 2026–27 Mission 3 theme: a motorized winch that lowers a towed sensor on two steel cables, switches the sensor lights through those cables, and reels it back into a dock. The work sits across mechanical design, electronics, and embedded control.

  • Mechanical Design: Printed frame, drum, and a streamlined sensor body sized for stable tow and reliable docking.
  • Electronics: Battery, motor driver, microcontrollers, and a slip ring so power and light commands ride the tow path.
  • Embedded Control: Encoder feedback for cable length, with commands from a laptop or an RC transmitter.
  • Goals: Deploy and retract to a set length, keep the sensor stable while towed, and switch light modes on command.
