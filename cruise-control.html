Coursework Project

Adaptive Cruise Control

Coursework (Team Project - Spring 2025)

Overview

Longitudinal adaptive cruise model: keep a safe, speed-dependent gap to a lead car. The study compared numerical integrators and tuned PID performance in MATLAB/Simulink.

What I Did

01 | System Dynamics

Plant

Modeled cruise, acceleration, and emergency braking (0.8g) regimes.

02 | Control Architecture

PID

2-second gap rule with tuned gains (Kp=0.5, Kd=0.8) for response without harsh oscillation.

03 | Numerical Integration

Solvers

Compared Euler vs adaptive ODE45; ODE45 held up better in transients.

04 | Robustness Analysis

Checks

Tested sensor latency and sinusoidal lead speeds against comfort limits.

Scope | Controls Coursework

Simulation study of longitudinal ACC. Focus on plant modeling, PID tuning, and integrator comparison.
