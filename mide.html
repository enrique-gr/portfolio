>> Overview

The Missouri Institute for Defense and Energy is a multidisciplinary research institute that bridges academia and industry to tackle complex defense and energy challenges. With over 80 researchers spanning engineering, physics, and beyond, MIDE collaborates with the Department of Defense, the Office of Naval Research, and the National Science Foundation across a wide range of programs.

At MIDE I worked experimental unmanned aircraft as systems. I helped return vehicles to test-ready after structural work, supported flight-test operations, and used flight data to understand how configuration affected whether an aircraft was ready to fly. I also brought platforms up from hardware through calibration and bench checks, and verified that sensing, estimation, and actuation hung together on the vehicle. I did not write a new controller.
