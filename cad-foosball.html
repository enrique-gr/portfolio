Coursework Project

Regulation Foosball Table

Coursework (Team Project - Fall 2023)

Overview

Team CAD build of a regulation foosball table with a modular frame, legs, and rod stack. My focus was player-rod sub-assemblies and final system integration in SolidWorks.

What I Did

01 | Sub-System Design

CAD

Modeled player rods, end caps, and washers with bearing/bushing tolerances for smooth rotation.

02 | Assembly Management

Integration

Led final SolidWorks assembly mates across beech frame, aluminum legs, and steel rods.

03 | Material Selection

BOM

Specified aluminum casting, solid beech, and vinyl finish in the bill of materials.

04 | DFM & Standardization

DFM

Used standard McMaster-Carr hardware for repairability and manufacturable assembly.

Scope | Coursework CAD

Bottom-up 200+ part assembly with modular subsystems. Emphasis on mates, tolerances, and a buildable BOM.
