Role Focus

Systems engineering is how complex hardware stays coherent. My focus in this role is making sure requirements, interfaces, and tests line up so the final system behaves reliably in the real world.

At a high level: translate mission needs into clear requirements, coordinate integration across subsystems, and close the loop with verification—so “it works” becomes provably works.

  • Requirements & Interfaces: Define what success means, document assumptions, and control interface contracts so components integrate cleanly.
  • Integration & Bring-Up: Debug hardware–software interactions, isolate root causes, and reduce “unknown unknowns” during assembly and test.
  • Verification & Test: Build test plans, trace requirements to evidence, and validate performance against real constraints (latency, noise, tolerance, environment).

Deliverables & Impact

The goal is simple: reduce integration surprises and increase confidence during test. I measure progress by what becomes clearer, faster, and more repeatable.

  • Cleaner requirements with measurable pass/fail criteria.
  • Fewer integration regressions through controlled interfaces and validation gates.
  • Test artifacts engineers can reuse: procedures, checklists, and verification evidence.
  • Faster root-cause isolation using structured debugging and logs.
